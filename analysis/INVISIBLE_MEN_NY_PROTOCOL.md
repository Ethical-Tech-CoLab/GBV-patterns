# The Invisible Men of New York: collection protocol

Draft for CATW to sign before any forum is read. Adapted from the published method of *Die Unsichtbaren Männer* (Germany, 2018) and CATW's *From Impunity to Accountability* (New York, 2022). Public page: https://ethical-tech-colab.github.io/GBV-patterns/invisible-men.html

## Purpose

Make the demand side of the New York sex trade visible in the buyers' own words, region by region and year by year, set beside the arrest record. Patterns, not profiles.

## Sources

The eight boards named by CATW (2022) and the MIT Lincoln Laboratory roadmap for DHS (2019): BestGFE, USA Sex Guide, Erotic Monkey, RubMaps, The Erotic Review, ECCIE, TNA Board, UtopiaGuide (offline). Status is rechecked and dated at each collection round. Google Maps and Facebook reviews of known establishments were a source in Germany and may be added.

## Who reads

Named researchers with CATW's agreement (as of November 2026: Elly, Nora's contact). The repository does not log in to, crawl or scrape any board. Index-level counts from public pages are the only automated collection.

## What is recorded for each quote

- The buyer's words, verbatim, trimmed to the sentence or sentences that carry the meaning
- Board
- Post year (month if shown)
- Region (one of CATW's ten) and, if clear, city
- Setting: indoor (apartment, massage parlour, hotel, strip club, other) or outdoor
- Theme(s) from the 26-item sheet below
- A screenshot kept offline by the reader, never committed, for verification
- Date read, reader's initials

## What is stripped before a quote enters the dataset

Usernames and handles; provider names, working names and ad titles; venue names and addresses; phone numbers, URLs, prices; physical descriptions of the woman; any detail that, combined with region and year, could identify her. Racist and misogynist terms stay, because they are the point; names do not.

## What is never collected

Images from the boards. Provider advertisements. Point locations finer than city. Anything about a woman from a source other than the buyer's own sentence.

## Selection

As in Germany: quotes are chosen to show misogynistic thinking and violent tendencies, not sampled at random. When a new term appears, the board is searched to see how common it is, and the count goes into the word-frequency table. The page states this openly.

## Publication

Each quote is shown as a silhouette with a speech bubble, in the figure used across the site. No face is generated. Quotes are grouped by region and year on the buyers page and appear as stories in the Corridor. The sources page records the boards and the dates read. The enforcement layer sits beside the quotes; no ratio of arrests to posts is ever presented as an impunity rate.

## Review

CATW reviews every batch before publication and can withdraw any quote. Readers can flag a quote for removal at any time.
