Case study in how to prevent femicide in the sex trade: the Gilgo Beach murders case

In 2010, a search for Shannan Gilbert, a missing woman who had been bought and sold online, led to the discovery of ten other bodies on a stretch of Long Island's South Shore called Gilgo Beach. In a case that spans three decades, Rex Heuermann admitted to killing eight women. There may be more.

The timeline moves through the case from the first body to the guilty plea. Watch the years add up before an arrest, and how many warning signs came first.
