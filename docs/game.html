Chutes and ladders: Gilgo Beach

Thirty squares along the public record of the Gilgo Beach killings, 2007 to 2023. Roll, and land where the case landed. Ladders are the laws and systems that could have caught him sooner. Chutes are the failures that let thirteen years pass. The ladders are what New York can still build.

From policy to outcome

What the evidence on this site says each ladder does, drawn the way RAND draws gun policy: policies on the left, outcomes on the right, a ribbon where there is evidence, labelled by how strong it is.

Evidence is what is already cited on this site: Sweden's buyer share under the Equality Model (0.29% in six months), Nashville's 400+ buyer arrests against Manhattan's 107, Operation Losing Proposition's 156 arrests in three days, Brooklyn's fall from 279 to 28 patronizing arrests, the 23 countries whose law names femicide. "Limited" means one study or one city; "moderate" means a national series; "supportive" means several sources agree. Model: RAND, Gun Policy in America.

Stories: In memoriam: New York (Sex Industry Kills), real cases from the public record, and the ten regions of CATW's sex-buyers map with the enforcement notes from From Impunity to Accountability (2022). Data: corridor_stories.json. Figures are silhouettes, the same ones used across the site; no image of a person is generated. How buyer quotes will be collected: the method. The Swiss cheese model: Mind the Gaps (Southern California Law Review, 2025).
