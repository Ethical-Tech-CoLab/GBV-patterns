The invisible men of New York: method

Since 2013 the Invisible Men projects have done one thing: take the words sex buyers write about the women they buy, and put them where the public can read them. Britain first, then Canada, France, Israel, Italy, Australia and New Zealand, then Germany, where Die Unsichtbaren Männer collected more than 350 quotes from eight forums and published its method. New York joined the lineage in 2022 with CATW's From Impunity to Accountability, 141 reviews placed on a map. This page takes the German method section by section and sets out how the New York collection continues, with what is already known and what is still to be done.

2 · By what criteria the quotes are selected

Chosen to expose, then checked for regularity

The German quotes were chosen on purpose to show misogyny and violence, not sampled at random. When a new term appeared, the forums were searched to see how common it was, and more examples were taken where it recurred. The project says so plainly, and adds that even if such buyers were a fifth of the whole, they would still be a chronic risk to the women. New York keeps that stance and adds three rules the German project did not need, because New York's boards sit beside an open memorial and a public map.

Quote the buyer, never describe the woman.

Usernames, venue names, addresses, phone numbers, prices, physical descriptions and anything that could identify a provider are stripped before a quote is recorded. The buyer's sentence stays.

Region or city, never a venue.

CATW's 141 points sit at review-post locations. New quotes are placed no finer than that.

A silhouette, never a face.

The German project paired quotes with a silhouette and a speech bubble. New York does the same, in the figure used across this site. No image of a person is generated.

The full collection protocol, for CATW to sign before any reading begins, is in the repository: INVISIBLE_MEN_NY_PROTOCOL.md.

3 · How representative the quotes are

By year, by region, by setting

The German project showed three distributions: three quarters of its quotes came from 2015 to 2018, the quotes were spread across every state roughly in line with population, and 93% were indoor against 87% indoor in the national picture. Each quote is coded indoor (apartment, massage parlour, hotel, strip club) or outdoor (street, track). The Penn Track in East New York is the outdoor market that the public record names; the boards are expected to lean indoor, as Germany's did.

4 · Analysis

Twenty-six themes, adopted as New York's coding sheet

The German project groups its quotes under twenty-six themes, from boundary complaints to trafficking. New York adopts the same list unchanged so the two collections can be read side by side. Each quote gets at least one theme, a region, a year, a setting and a board.

Theme list: Die Unsichtbaren Männer, Analysis. The German project also published a word-frequency table: counts of selected terms on four forums in October 2018, grouped under appearance, character, ethnicities, women, intercourse, body parts, self-designation. New York will do the same once a researcher with forum access runs the searches; the term list starts from the words CATW's 2022 report found and grows with the collection.

What the collection needs

In order.

The protocol signed

CATW agrees the three rules above and the coding sheet. Until then nothing is read.

Readers with access

Four of the eight boards need an account or a browser. Nora's contact and Elly already read these boards; their exports feed the coder.

The coder and the cards

A script strips identifiers, assigns region, year, setting and theme, and flags anything that still names a person or place. A generator renders each quote as a silhouette and speech bubble for the buyers page and the Corridor.
