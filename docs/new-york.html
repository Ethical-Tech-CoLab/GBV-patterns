The Empire State to be first in the U.S. to define femicide in law

No US state defines femicide. New York has the data, the courts and the advocates to be first. This page is the case: the women the state does not count, the buyers it does not arrest, and what the law would change.

The buyers

The demand side is not hidden. In 2022 CATW geolocated 141 reviews that sex buyers posted about the women they bought, across ten regions of the state, and set them against an arrest record that had collapsed: Brooklyn made 279 patronizing arrests in 2018 and 28 in 2020. The full map, region by region, with the impunity ledger, is on its own page.
