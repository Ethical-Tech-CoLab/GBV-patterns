Femicide in the sex trade

The killing of women in prostitution and sex trafficking, by buyers, pimps and traffickers, and the deaths the trade causes more slowly. No agency in New York counts them.

Why it is femicide

CATW text: the killing of women in the sex trade as gender-based killing; the legal definitions that include it abroad.

How women die in the trade

CATW text and sources: homicide by buyers and exploiters; overdose, exposure, untreated injury and illness; the long-term toll.

Why no one counts

CATW text: classification as overdose or accident, "relationship unknown", media language. See the ledger's blind spot on the home page.
