Economic impact

If lives do not move a legislature, money might. France was persuaded by a cost study. This page summarises it, then counts what New York loses.

What a life costs, in lost earnings

A woman killed at thirty in New York loses fifty-one years of life and thirty-five years of work. Move the slider. The figures use the state's median earnings for women working full time and United States life expectancy for women, without discounting or wage growth, so they are conservative.

What other countries have put a number on

Where governments and development banks have costed violence against women, the figures land between a third of a percent and a tenth of GDP, depending on what is counted. None of these studies is directly comparable with another, and each says its estimate is conservative.

0.31% of GDP
cost of killings of women in Latin America, more than double the global average of 0.12%. Inter-American Development Bank, October 2026.
