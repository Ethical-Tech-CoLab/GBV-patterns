New York › Sex buyers

The buyers are visible. The arrests are not.

In 2022 CATW read hundreds of posts on "hobby boards" where sex buyers across New York State review the women they bought, and placed each one on a map. The demand side of the sex trade is not hidden; it writes reviews. This page rebuilds that map as an open layer and sets it against the arrest record, which is the measure of impunity.

Content warning: the original reviews are violent, racist and degrading. They are not reproduced here. Each point marks only where a review was posted from. The full report, with the quotes, is linked below.

Impunity, in numbers

The report's own measure: how many buyers there are, against how many are ever arrested.

Brooklyn: arrests for patronizing, 2018 to 2020

Brooklyn's DA stopped prosecuting prostitution in 2021 but kept prosecuting patronizing. Arrests had already collapsed. Pimping arrests fell from 36 to 7 over the same years.

What this layer makes possible

Three overlays, in order of readiness, that turn the map from a picture of demand into a measure of impunity.

Arrests per review, by region

Add patronizing and promoting arrests by county from the New York State Division of Criminal Justice Services, and show arrests per hundred reviews. The regions with the most reviews and the fewest arrests are the impunity map.

Deaths on the same map

Once Women Count USA aggregation is agreed, place women killed in the sex trade on this layer. The buyers' reviews and the women's deaths occupy the same streets.
