New York

2024 Annual Fact Sheet

NYC Mayor's Office to End Domestic and Gender-Based Violence (ENDGBV), published 3 October 2025
Verify
66 domestic violence homicides in 2024 (33 intimate partner, 33 family); 249,077 domestic incident reports (116,051 intimate partner, 92,884 family, 40,142 unknown); borough tables for 2023 and 2024; 57,656 Family Justice Center visits; hotline calls 87,985 in 2023 and 81,949 in 2024.

2025 Annual Report on Domestic Violence Initiatives, Indicators and Factors

ENDGBV, Local Law 38 report covering 2025
Verify
Not yet used for figures. 2025 totals to be added when extracted.

2023 Annual Report

ENDGBV
Verify
Homicide series: 62 in 2021, 71 in 2022, 73 in 2023 (73 is derived from the 2024 fact sheet's 2023 columns: 30 intimate partner plus 43 family).

2024 New York State Gender-Based Violence Dashboard

NYS Office for the Prevention of Domestic Violence, November 2025. Interactive Tableau version linked.
Verify
241,351 orders of protection; 87,417 and 167,551 hotline calls; 8,289 state hotline and 11,676 national hotline calls; 10,227 residential admissions; 24,635 shelter denials (12,696 adults, 11,939 children); 115 and 44 day average stays; 67,934 family offense petitions; 10,087 extreme risk orders; 38 intimate partner homicides outside NYC (32 women, 4 men, 2 unknown). PDF brief.

Global

Femicides in 2024: global estimates of intimate partner and family member femicides

UNODC and UN Women, November 2025
Verify
About 50,000 women and girls killed by a partner or family member in 2024; about 137 a day; 60% of all intentional killings of women; 117 countries; 2023 estimate 51,100.

Violence against women prevalence estimates, 2018

Verify
