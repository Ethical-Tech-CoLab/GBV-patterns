Economics and demography

What is the Economic Cost of Violence Against Women?

Andrea Saldarriaga Jimenez and Paula Kantor, Inter-American Development Bank blog, 7 October 2026. CC BY 4.0.
Verify
One in four women in Latin America and the Caribbean has experienced partner violence; cost of killings of women 0.31% of regional GDP against 0.12% globally; Paraguay 5.12% and Ecuador 4.3% of GDP; Spain €4,933 million, 0.37%; South Africa 0.9% to 1.3%; Argentina 0.8% to 2.1% by bottom-up accounting, with its exclusions listed.

Economic toll of GBV: Kenya loses Sh41 billion annually, taskforce finds

Kamau Maichuhie, Daily Nation, 19 June 2025. Reporting on the Technical Working Group on Gender-Based Violence, Including Femicide, chaired by Dr Nancy Baraza, and Kenyatta University's Women's Economic Empowerment Hub.
Verify
Sh41 billion a year; GDP losses 7.8% to 10%; 725 femicides in 2024, 2.66 per 100,000, 47 a week, 75% by someone known; 35% of women and girls affected; per-survivor care costs Sh109,551 in Nakuru and Sh111,113 in Makueni; 2016 study Sh46 billion, 1.1% of GDP; developing-country range 1.2% to 3.7% of GDP. verify Newspaper summary; taskforce report and UNODC figure to be checked at source.
